## Final answer requirements

Specify:

- next primary objective;
- why it dominates alternatives now;
- exact artifact, probe, or reconstruction to produce;
- what result would be genuinely surprising;
- stopping conditions;
- materially different outcome branches;
- what must stay frozen;
- what should explicitly not be done yet.

Prefer the move with the greatest expected reduction of consequential uncertainty, not the move that produces the most material.
