# 03 — bounded decision

Read the repository state plus the outputs of Pass A and Pass B.

Select exactly one primary objective for the next bounded research cycle.

Your decision should state:

- **Objective** — one sentence.
- **Why now** — why this dominates the alternatives at this point in the round.
- **Exact work product** — the probe, reconstruction, comparison, coordinate challenge, or revision artifact to produce.
- **Evidence plan** — what evidence or verifier matters and what does not.
- **Surprising result** — what outcome would materially change the current map.
- **Stopping condition** — when to stop this cycle even if more material is available.
- **Outcome branches** — what to do if the result supports, weakens, or fails to discriminate the target claim.
- **Frozen items** — what must not change during this cycle.
- **Not now** — important work that should explicitly wait.

Also classify the chosen move as primarily one of: search, representation, discrimination, integration, or method. If the taxonomy does not fit, explain why.

Do not hedge by assigning several equal primary objectives. The purpose of this pass is allocation.

End with a compact `NEXT CYCLE` block that the user can hand directly back to the research system.
