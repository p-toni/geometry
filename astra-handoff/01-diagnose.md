# 01 — diagnosis

Review the research state using `00-manifest.md`.

Produce four sections:

1. **Epistemic state** — what is supported, failed, ambiguous, taxonomic, and unresolved.
2. **Current bottleneck** — choose the primary bottleneck among search, representation, discrimination, integration, or method. Add another category only if necessary.
3. **Candidate next moves** — compare 3–5 genuinely different moves. For each, state the uncertainty reduced, likely information value, cost, framing risk, and what result would change your view.
4. **Provisional choice** — choose one next move for one bounded research cycle and state the condition that would make you choose differently.

Include at least one candidate based on a concrete probe or construction rather than more reading, and at least one candidate that reduces protocol overhead.

Do not assume Geometry or the current protocol should be preserved. Prefer reduction of consequential uncertainty over production of additional artifacts.

Complete this pass before reading the later pass instructions.
