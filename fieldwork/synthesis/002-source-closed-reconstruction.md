## What we are still entitled to carry

Four things survive.

First, adjacent capabilities should not be inferred from one another without conditions. Observation, prediction, intervention, control, verification, and revision can separate. This is a useful distinction with narrow support, not a new universal theory.

Second, post-hoc fit is not explanation. An equivalence class, constraint, or accessibility story earns explanatory status only when its criterion or mechanism has enough independent content that some alternatives would fail. This is currently best treated as an epistemic rule for inquiry.

Third, explanatory level and boundary must be declared. Function, mechanism, implementation, system boundary, and verifier boundary can vary independently enough that cross-domain resemblance is unsafe without indexing the claim.

Fourth, Geometry remains ontologically open. Round 001 has not selected among constructed, dynamically selected, discovered, accessed, or distributed structure. The plural list remains useful because no unification has been earned.

## What does not survive as active theory

The original seed chain does not survive. The working backbone also no longer survives as a single explanatory object. Several clauses failed or became non-discriminating as written; one is taxonomic; one is a narrow separation claim; one remains ambiguous.

Therefore **there is currently no unified general Geometry theory entitled to active commitment from round 001**.

What remains is a scoped toolkit for avoiding false synthesis:

- state the relevant capacity relation rather than invoking boundedness alone;
- specify what an equivalence preserves before seeing the desired partition;
- require mechanisms to rule out alternatives rather than redescribe outcomes;
- keep capability, level, boundary, and verification distinctions explicit;
- preserve ontology choices until contact discriminates them.

This toolkit may later support a theory. It is not itself evidence that one exists.

## Prospective-test decision

**No new theoretical test is currently earned.**

The strongest retained substantive item — separation of prediction, intervention, and control — is already a scoped distinction and is not uniquely a Geometry claim. The quarantined repairs add premises that have not earned promotion. The learning/error clause is not operational enough for a clean discriminator. The coordinate challenge remains potentially important but lacks a specific case whose missing observable blocks a live decision.

The next move should not manufacture a test merely to preserve momentum. A later cycle can open only when a specific substantive claim, missing coordinate, or decision consequence is stated strongly enough that an outcome could count against it.
