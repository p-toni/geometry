## Why the round closes here

Round 001 has reached a stable stopping condition. The post-falsification reconstruction found no unified general theory entitled to active commitment and no new theoretical test currently earned. Continuing to search for a replacement chain would violate the protocol's own stop rule.

The round therefore closes by **subtracting commitments**, not by publishing a new thesis.

## What round 001 established

The strongest outcomes are negative and discriminating:

- boundedness alone does not imply incomplete representation, observation, or realization;
- post-hoc accessibility stories and preservation criteria can be non-discriminating unless their mechanisms are independently specified;
- prediction, intervention, and control can dissociate and should not be inferred from one another without conditions;
- taxonomies and inquiry guards must not be promoted as though they were general laws;
- a repaired claim is a new claim, not survival of the failed predecessor;
- a useful research protocol should be invoked selectively because ordinary research is better for some low-conflation tasks.

## What survives

A scoped toolkit for research:

1. state relative capacity instead of invoking boundedness alone;
2. predeclare what a representation/equivalence must preserve;
3. require explanations to rule out alternatives rather than fit outcomes after the fact;
4. index claims by capability, explanatory level, system boundary, and verifier boundary where those distinctions matter;
5. keep ontology open until contact discriminates alternatives.

This toolkit is not a Geometry theory.

## What is withdrawn from active commitment

The frozen seed remains the historical starting object, but its inferential chain is not carried forward as theory.

The first working backbone also closes as an experimental object. Its clauses ended the round with different statuses: failed universal, non-discriminating formulations, taxonomy, scoped distinction, and unresolved ambiguity. They should not be silently recomposed into another chain.

## Open but inactive

The round leaves many interesting branches unresolved. They are not debts that must be paid before moving on. They may be reopened only when a new native question or decision makes them consequential.

Examples include:

- whether changing observables is sometimes the correct description of novelty rather than movement inside a fixed state space;
- when local guarantees compose;
- when traceability is required for reliable contact;
- whether tetrahedral/simplicial decompositions are unusually natural in particular systems;
- how to operationalize learning without making `error` true by definition.

None currently earns a deep cycle merely by being open.
