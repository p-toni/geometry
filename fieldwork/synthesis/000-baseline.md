# synthesis 000 — baseline before divergence

Status: **not source-closed**  
Purpose: handoff into round 001, not a conclusion.

## What appears load-bearing at the start

- boundedness is real for the human learner;
- useful representations require selective loss, equivalence, or constraint;
- ownership and access are not the same thing;
- a map cannot certify itself solely by internal coherence;
- error/feedback needs somewhere to return and change the system;
- coupling to AI increases both reachable possibility and closure pressure.

## What is no longer safe to assume

- that compression is the unique or central bridge from structure to prediction/control;
- that invariants necessarily precede persistence rather than being recognized through it;
- that the learner is always an individual human;
- that 'retrieval' is a single phenomenon;
- that closure is only an epistemic failure mode;
- that interface means only a boundary or affordance rather than a selector of reachable possibilities.

## What remains deliberately unresolved

Constructed vs discovered vs accessed structure; immanent vs Platonic possibility; individual vs distributed knowing; what counts as contact across different domains; when geometric language explains rather than redescribes.

Round 001 starts by widening these uncertainties, not resolving them.
