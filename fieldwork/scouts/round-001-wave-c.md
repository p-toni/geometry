## Promotion — first six

Wave C promotes six cells for sustained native investigation:

1. **relevant-information-under-bottleneck** — information theory;
2. **coarse-graining-and-universality** — statistical physics;
3. **partial-observation-and-control** — control theory;
4. **transitions-in-individuality** — evolutionary biology;
5. **cultural-transmission-and-structure** — language evolution;
6. **convergence-without-synchrony** — distributed systems.

The selection criterion was diversity of native mechanism and discriminating potential, not similarity to the seed. Other cells stay on the frontier and can be promoted when these six stop producing new distinctions or when a collision demands them.

## Scout discipline

A scout report is allowed to say “no branch here.” Dead ends are successful exploration when they shrink the useful frontier.
