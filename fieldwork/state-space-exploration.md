# state-space exploration

This file defines a **research operating model**, not a claim about the ontology of minds, mathematics, or reality.

The working premise is simple:

> A bounded learner can build systems that explore vastly more possibilities than the learner ever has to carry.

The research system should therefore optimize for **wide exploration with narrow human-facing load**.

## Units

- **frontier** — candidate question-spaces not yet investigated deeply;
- **scout** — a cheap, source-native exploration of one frontier cell;
- **branch** — a question-space that earned sustained investigation;
- **source card** — one provenance-preserving native pass;
- **collision** — pressure created by independently developed branches;
- **probe** — an external or formal constraint that can discriminate among live alternatives;
- **progress unit** — a new question, distinction, discriminating consequence, intervention, or claim we can no longer honestly hold.

Source count is not progress.

## Exploration budget

Default budget for a broad pulse:

```text
24–40 blind scouts
      ↓
8–14 sustained native branches
      ↓
2–4 live collisions in the integrator working set
      ↓
0–2 probes promoted at a time
      ↓
1 source-closed reconstruction
```

The numbers are operating ranges, not quotas. Increase or decrease them when the quality of independence or native understanding starts to fall.

## Three populations

### 1. Blind scouts

A scout receives a native question and enough domain context to search responsibly. It does **not** receive:

- `fieldwork/seed.md`;
- the current collision map;
- the published essays;
- instructions to find support or opposition for Geometry.

Its job is to return only:

- what the domain calls the problem;
- one or two strong entry sources;
- the important internal disagreement, if visible;
- whether a deeper branch appears warranted.

Blindness is procedural. It reduces convergence pressure; it does not imply true cognitive independence between model runs.

### 2. Native investigators

Promoted branches get enough depth to become internally legible. They can see related source cards but should still avoid the seed until the native account is written.

A branch is promoted because it contains an unresolved mechanism, distinction, anomaly, or empirical program—not because it resembles our vocabulary.

### 3. Integrators

Integrators see the seed and independently developed branches late. Their job is to identify **displacement**, not to create a unified theory.

The integrator working set should stay small: normally two to four collisions at once.

## Promotion rule

A scout can become a branch when at least one is true:

- the domain contains a distinction we cannot yet express cleanly;
- competing native explanations predict different outcomes;
- a mechanism is relevant to a live question but unlike our current mechanism;
- the literature contains an anomaly or intervention worth tracing;
- the branch could falsify or sharply limit a current assumption;
- the branch is sufficiently alien that deeper study is a useful independence test.

Semantic resemblance is not a promotion reason.

## Human carrying budget

The search frontier can expand much faster than the update shown to the human.

Default human-facing update:

- at most **three to five displacements**;
- no literature dump;
- dead branches are summarized statistically unless one failure is itself informative;
- unresolved alternatives remain unresolved.

The research system carries the frontier. The learner carries the consequences.

## Diversity pressure

Do not let search become one large family of neighboring literatures. Across a broad wave, deliberately include multiple kinds of systems:

- living / evolutionary;
- physical / dynamical;
- mathematical / formal;
- engineered / computational;
- social / institutional;
- perceptual / cognitive;
- at least a few domains selected without obvious relevance.

This is not an ontology. It is a defense against local search.

## Stop / reset conditions

Pause expansion and run source-closed reconstruction when any of these appear:

- new branches mostly rename distinctions already found;
- source cards become shorter because the conclusion feels predictable;
- the same small set of authors or vocabularies dominates multiple branches;
- collisions increase only by semantic analogy;
- the human-facing displacement has stopped changing despite large search spend;
- one cheap discriminating probe has become more informative than further reading.

After reconstruction, either promote probes, open a fresh blind wave, or close the round.
