## Thesis v0

> A bounded learner cannot carry the whole world.  
> So it searches for invariants.  
> Invariants induce symmetries.  
> Symmetries make compression possible.  
> Compression makes prediction and control possible.  
> Prediction and control only matter if the world is allowed to answer.

This is the current coordinate system, not a result to defend.

Candidate verbal form:

No such structure certifies itself: an answer must be able to return and change either the geometry or the interface that produced it.

## Live tensions

- Is compression central, or only one way bounded systems gain leverage over complexity?

- What counts as contact in mathematics, biology, software, introspection, and institutions?
