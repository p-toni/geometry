# collision — invariants without representation

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `chong-proctor-2020-affordances` — Gibson's ecological psychology treats some perceptual information as directly available in the organism–environment relation rather than reconstructed as an internal representation.
- `brooks-1991-intelligence-without-representation` — situated agents can achieve competent control without a central explicit world model.
- the frozen seed — begins with a bounded learner searching for invariants.

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

The word `invariant` can tempt us into assuming an internal abstraction: the learner notices repeated structure, compresses it, and stores a map. Gibson gives a different possibility. Some invariance may be in the structured environmental information available to an organism, and competent action may consist in becoming sensitive to it rather than reconstructing it internally.

Brooks adds an engineering case in which environment-coupling can replace part of the burden assigned to a central representation.

## New distinction / contradiction

Keep separate:

1. **environmental invariance** — structure stable in organism-relevant information;
2. **detected invariance** — a system's behavior becomes sensitive to that structure;
3. **represented invariance** — the structure is encoded in an internal state that can be decoupled from the immediate environment;
4. **owned/reconstructable invariance** — a learner can deliberately rebuild and reason from it.

The first two do not entail the latter two.

## Pressure on the seed

“Searches for invariants” currently leaves the location and mechanism of the invariant unspecified. That ambiguity may be useful, but only if we resist silently converting detection into representation.

This is a direct challenge to any formulation in which geometry must live inside the learner.

## What remains unresolved

Where direct coupling stops being sufficient—offline reasoning, counterfactual planning, mathematics, delayed re-entry, and communication may require forms of representation that immediate perception/action does not.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Choose a task that can be solved both online and after the relevant environment is removed. Compare what an ecological/direct-coupling account and an internal-representation account predict about transfer, delay, novel recombination, and counterfactual use.

The point is not to prove one worldview globally, but to identify where the burden of useful structure actually moves from environment to internal state.
