# collision — contact needs traceability

Status: **open**

## Independently developed inputs

- `contact-is-mediated`: external difference can fail to survive a measurement channel;
- BIPM metrology: a measurement result is traceable through a documented calibration chain, with uncertainty contributed along the chain;
- formal verification: a proof certificate is only meaningful relative to the statement, assumptions, checker, and trusted base;
- science studies: observations become transportable claims through instruments, inscriptions, procedures, and checking practices.

## Displacement

“World contact” is not one edge. It is a path whose transformations can add uncertainty, erase distinctions, or change what the result licenses.

A stronger form is emerging:

```text
external difference
→ interaction
→ measurement / transformation
→ reference relation
→ discrimination
→ verification
→ revision boundary
```

Each arrow is a possible failure surface.

## Distinction created

```text
contact
≠ traceable contact
≠ discriminating contact
≠ decision-adequate contact
```

Traceability itself does not guarantee sufficient precision or absence of mistakes.

## What this does not establish

Metrological traceability should not be universalized into a literal calibration chain for every form of knowledge. The cross-domain candidate is only that consequential evidence requires an inspectable relation between external constraint and the claim/action it is used to justify.

## Next discriminator

Compare a metrological chain, a formal-proof chain, and one qualitative scientific inference. Ask what minimal structure survives once domain-specific notions of calibration are removed.
