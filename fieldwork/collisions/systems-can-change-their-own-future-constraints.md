# collision — systems can change their own future constraints

Status: **open**

## Independently developed inputs

- path dependence: earlier choices change later payoffs and can create lock-in;
- niche construction: organism activity can alter later selection pressures and ecological inheritance;
- market information: public prices/summaries can alter incentives to generate the information they summarize;
- iterated learning: transmitted representations become the input environment for later learners.

## Displacement

The effective state space is not always merely a fixed space with biased traversal. In some systems, action changes the transition/payoff/selection structure that will govern later states.

This suggests at least three different notions:

```text
move within a fixed space
change which regions are reachable
change the rules that shape future reachability
```

The third is stronger than ordinary exploration.

## What this does not establish

It does not imply that agents freely design their own possibility spaces or that every feedback loop constitutes self-construction. The relevant change must alter future constraints, transition structure, or selection pressures in a way that matters to subsequent trajectories.

## Next discriminator

Use one biological and one engineered case to distinguish ordinary state transition from endogenous modification of the transition rule. Identify whether the same formal object can represent both without hiding the causal difference.
