## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

The sources place the missing structure in different locations.

**Levin:** structured patterns may exist independently of embodiment; embodiments act as interfaces/pointers that enable their ingression.

**Lee Smart:** possibility may be immanent to one underlying reality; boundaries and dynamics make some possibilities admissible, selection chooses among them, and closure stabilizes persistent form.

**Hartl et al.:** adaptive systems can be modeled as remapping and navigating effective embedding spaces under error minimization, without requiring Levin's stronger ontology.

**Our seed:** begins from the bounded learner and asks how it forms usable structure and remains answerable to what exceeds it.

These positions cannot be merged by saying they all describe 'geometry'.

## New distinctions exposed

1. **possible** — a state/form can in some sense occur;
2. **accessible** — this system/interface can reach or enact it;
3. **represented** — it is encoded or modeled somewhere in the system;
4. **selected** — dynamics actually move toward/choose it;
5. **persistent** — it remains stable enough to matter over time;
6. **owned** — a learner can reconstruct/navigate enough of it to use intentionally.

The current essays blur several of these.

## Pressure on the seed

The old chain puts invariants and compression early. This collision suggests they might instead be consequences/signatures of persistence or tools for access, not universal prerequisites.

It also splits 'closure':

- **generative closure** — enough stabilization for a form/process to persist;
- **epistemic closure** — treating a representation as more final than evidence warrants.

## What we do not know

- whether `possible → accessible → selected → persistent` is a real causal sequence or only a useful bookkeeping order;
- whether accessibility requires internal representation;
- whether persistence is sufficient to generate recognizable invariants/symmetries;
- whether any observation distinguishes Levin's pattern-space from strong immanent dynamical accounts;
- where the relevant boundary lies in distributed AI systems.

## Candidate probes

Do not promote these yet. They need sharpening.

- Find a biological case where two theories predict different reachable forms under the same perturbation.
- Find a formal/computational case where the same local rules yield different persistent high-level structures depending on boundary/interface conditions, and ask what is actually represented.
- Use a distributed proof system to separate `verified`, `reconstructable by a human`, and `actionable by a human` as measurable states.
