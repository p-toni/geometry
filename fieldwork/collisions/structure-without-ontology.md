# collision — structure without ontology

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `levin-2026-ingressing-minds` — mathematical patterns motivate a stronger hypothesis in which a structured non-physical pattern space causally in-forms physical embodiments.
- `reck-schiemer-2025-mathematical-structuralism` — structural mathematical practice is compatible with several mutually incompatible metaphysical positions, including realist *ante rem* structure and nominalist “structuralism without structures.”

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

Both take mathematical structure seriously, but they disagree about what that seriousness licenses. Levin uses mathematics as an entry point to an ontological expansion. Structuralist philosophy shows that representation-independent or role-based structure does not uniquely determine such an ontology.

The shared word “structure” therefore hides the disagreement rather than resolving it.

## New distinction / contradiction

Keep these separate:

- **structural usefulness** — reasoning by relations/roles works;
- **structural invariance** — results survive changes of representation or realization;
- **structural objectivity** — constraints are not chosen freely by an observer;
- **structural ontology** — structures exist independently in some specified sense;
- **causal ingression** — such structure contributes causal specificity to physical events.

The first three do not automatically entail the last two.

## Pressure on the seed

This is currently protective rather than destructive. Geometry can remain an operational research object without acquiring a metaphysics. But any future move from “we keep finding stable structure” to “there is a pre-existing space of structure” now has an explicit missing argument.

## What remains unresolved

Levin intends the strong ontology to earn its place through a research program and novel capabilities, not mathematical analogy alone. The real question is therefore whether that program eventually generates discriminating predictions that weaker structural/dynamical accounts do not.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Choose one empirical phenomenon Levin presents as especially difficult for genetics/environment/mechanism alone. Write the strongest conventional dynamical/evolutionary account and the strongest Platonic-ingression account *before* examining further data. Ask what observation or intervention would differ between them.

If no discriminating consequence can be stated, the ontological addition remains unearned even if it is heuristically fertile.
