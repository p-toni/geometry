# collision — state space may not be prestated

Status: **open**

## Inputs

- research operating model: state-space exploration;
- path dependence / niche construction;
- Longo–Montévil–Kauffman on changing biological phase spaces.

## Collision

The protocol already distinguishes a nominal frontier from an effective frontier shaped by history and search. That still assumes that the relevant coordinates or kinds of possibility are available in principle.

The hostile biological account claims something stronger: evolution can generate new relevant observables, phenotypes, niches, and boundary conditions that were not prestated as coordinates of the earlier phase space.

If that is even sometimes operationally correct, “exploration” has at least four different forms:

1. locate an unknown state in a known space;
2. change which known states are reachable;
3. change transition rules / constraints;
4. create or recognize new dimensions / observables by which later states can be described.

## Pressure

A research system that only allocates more scouts over an inherited coordinate system can miss category invention. Fresh starts reduce path dependence but do not guarantee new coordinates.

## Candidate probe

Take one historical scientific transition where a new measurable variable or category became necessary. Reconstruct whether the later discovery can be expressed cleanly as search over the earlier representation without smuggling the later coordinate back into the earlier model.

If not, the protocol needs a generative-frontier mechanism in addition to coverage and accessibility controls.
