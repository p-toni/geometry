# collision — contact is mediated

Status: **open**

## Independently formed inputs

- `mediated-scientific-contact` — observations become portable evidence through instruments, inscriptions, routines, and argument;
- `verification-has-multiple-boundaries` — derivation validity, statement fidelity, assumptions, checker trust, and uptake are distinct;
- `intervention-and-identifiability` — more observation through the same channel can fail to discriminate competing models;
- `representation-changes-its-source` — public representations can alter incentives and therefore the future evidence stream.

## Displacement

“The world answers” is too coarse if it hides the **answer channel**.

A useful contact event has at least:

```text
world/process
   ↓
interaction or intervention
   ↓
measurement / inscription
   ↓
transport / transformation
   ↓
verification / comparison
   ↓
error lands somewhere
```

Failure can occur at any edge. A perfectly real external constraint can become epistemically useless if the channel erases the distinction under test.

## New distinction

- **external constraint exists**;
- **the interface is sensitive to it**;
- **the signal survives transport/transformation**;
- **a verifier can discriminate it**;
- **the result reaches a boundary that can revise behavior**.

These are not equivalent.

## Pressure on the corpus

Contact may need to be represented as a path with failure surfaces, not a single terminal node. This remains a candidate until it survives source-closed reconstruction and heterogeneous contact.

## What remains unresolved

Mediation does not imply social construction in the strong sense. The rival realist reading—that instruments and institutions are precisely how bounded investigators gain reliable access to an external world—remains fully live.
