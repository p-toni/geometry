# collision — persistence without mechanism identity

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `mccolgan-difrisco-2024-developmental-system-drift` — homologous phenotype can persist while its genetic developmental underpinnings diverge.
- `argote-guo-2016-routines-transactive-memory` — organizational knowledge can persist through routines that tolerate member turnover, while other memory mechanisms depend strongly on particular members.
- `hutchins-1991-organizing-work-by-adaptation` — a work configuration can stabilize through local adaptations before participants explicitly represent the global solution.

These come from evo-devo, organizational learning, and cognitive ethnography respectively.

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

All three involve persistence, but the thing that persists is different: phenotype, repeated coordination, or system-level work configuration. Treating them as one generic “stable pattern” would erase the most useful difference.

The collision is that **observable continuity does not tell us which underlying continuity is doing the explanatory work**. The same endpoint can survive substantial causal turnover; conversely, a system can preserve many parts yet lose the coordination that made it functionally the same.

## New distinction / contradiction

At least four senses of persistence now need to be kept separate:

1. **output persistence** — the same recognizable result recurs;
2. **implementation persistence** — the same causal machinery remains;
3. **coordination persistence** — relations among changing parts preserve a capability;
4. **identity persistence** — an observer or practice continues treating the system as the same entity.

A fifth candidate is **counterfactual persistence**: whether two apparently identical systems continue behaving alike when pushed away from the conditions under which the similarity was observed.

## Pressure on the seed

“Invariant” is currently under-specified. An invariant at one level can be purchased by drift at another. If we do not name the level and transformation class, “searches for invariants” risks mistaking a stable face for a stable mechanism.

This is not yet a reason to remove invariants from the seed. It is a reason to make them expensive: *invariant of what, under what perturbation, and with what hidden degrees of freedom allowed to drift?*

## What remains unresolved

Whether counterfactual persistence is the right discriminator, and whether there is a principled way to choose the level at which persistence matters without merely choosing the level that makes our preferred story work.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Take one well-characterized DSD case and one organizational routine that survives member turnover. For each, compare two systems that look equivalent at the preserved-output level but differ in implementation. Apply or identify a perturbation under which their responses diverge.

If divergence appears reliably, endpoint invariance is insufficient; the research system needs a notion closer to **counterfactual structure** than visible persistence.
