# collision — the state space is shaped by dynamics

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `onuchic-luthey-schulten-wolynes-1997-energy-landscape` — protein conformational space is not explored uniformly; energetic bias and frustration structure which trajectories are accessible and likely.
- `arthur-1989-increasing-returns-lock-in` — earlier choices can alter the payoff landscape facing later choices, producing self-reinforcing trajectories and lock-in.
- `kirby-cornish-smith-2008-iterated-learning` — repeated transmission through bounded learners changes the structure of the object being transmitted.

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

The mechanisms are different. Protein folding involves a physicochemical energy landscape; technology adoption involves endogenous economic payoffs; cultural transmission changes the artifact through repeated reconstruction. But all three reject a neutral picture in which a fixed set of possibilities is merely sampled.

The collision is methodological: **reachability is itself structured and can be historically altered**.

## New distinction / contradiction

Keep separate:

- **logical possibility** — a state can be described;
- **physical accessibility** — dynamics can reach it under feasible trajectories;
- **economic/institutional accessibility** — path-dependent payoffs make it viable;
- **cultural accessibility** — repeated transmission preserves or transforms it;
- **search accessibility** — the research process is likely to encounter it given its current branch history.

A large nominal state space can therefore have a very small effective frontier, and the effective frontier can change over time.

## Pressure on the research system

“State-space exploration” must not imply uniform sampling from a fixed space. Our own search can become path-dependent as prior sources shape vocabulary, salience and branch promotion.

Blind scouts are therefore not only anti-confirmation controls; they are **anti-lock-in mechanisms**. Periodic fresh-start waves should be treated as part of the exploration architecture.

## Pressure on the seed

If this survives, the sequence may need something more basic than boundedness → representation. The learner meets a landscape whose transition structure already constrains what can be encountered, and its own actions can reshape that landscape.

## What remains unresolved

Whether a general formal notion of accessibility can span these domains without collapsing very different mechanisms into one metaphor.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Run matched scout batches on the same set of native questions under two conditions: fresh-context isolation versus exposure to the current Geometry collision map. Predeclare measures of source overlap, vocabulary overlap, domain diversity, and number of genuinely new distinctions. If seeded scouts converge more strongly on existing vocabulary without improving native evidence quality, path dependence is operationally visible in the research process itself.
