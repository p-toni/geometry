# collision — minimum system does not imply universal geometry

Status: **open**

## Inputs

- Fuller / Synergetics: tetrahedron as minimum structural system, triangulation as stability primitive, tetrahedral coordinate system;
- Chern: geometry changes meaning across transformation groups, manifolds, bundles, connections, curvature;
- Amari / information geometry: statistical models carry intrinsic geometry through Fisher information and related structures.

## Collision

A real minimality result can be mistaken for a universal ontology.

The tetrahedron is the 3-simplex and the minimum convex 3D polyhedral enclosure. Triangulation is also an important structural technique. But neither fact by itself establishes that every useful geometry, every stable information structure, or every physical system is intrinsically tetrahedral.

This forces four layers apart:

1. **minimum carrier** — the simplest object satisfying a chosen representation/enclosure requirement;
2. **stable construction** — a structure whose mechanics benefit from triangulation;
3. **coordinate primitive** — a convenient basis or decomposition scheme;
4. **intrinsic / universal geometry** — a claim about what structure actually governs the phenomenon independent of representation.

The first three can be true while the fourth is false or unearned.

## Why it pressures Geometry

Our own project is vulnerable to the same move. A geometry that is useful for organizing bounded cognition could become a universal explanatory substrate merely because it is elegant, minimal, or repeatedly reusable.

The hostile question is therefore not “is tetrahedral geometry useful?” but:

> What observation or theorem distinguishes “a powerful representational primitive” from “the intrinsic geometry that rules the phenomenon”?

## Candidate discriminator

Use information geometry as a control. Statistical manifolds can possess intrinsic dimension, curvature, metric, and connection structure that are defined from probability models. If a tetrahedral representation is imposed, ask whether predictions/invariants depend on that tetrahedralization or survive coordinate/triangulation changes.

If all empirically relevant quantities are invariant to the chosen simplicial representation, tetrahedrality is a carrier, not the intrinsic explanatory geometry.

## Current disposition

Do not promote “tetrahedron rules them all.” Preserve Fuller's minimum-system result and engineering insights while requiring a separate certificate for every stronger physical or informational universality claim.
