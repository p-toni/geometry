# collision — persistence without a target

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `mccolgan-difrisco-2024-developmental-system-drift` — a conserved phenotype can persist while developmental implementation changes.
- `montevil-mossio-2015-closure-of-constraints` — biological organization can be characterized through mutually maintained constraints in a thermodynamically open system.
- `levin-2026-ingressing-minds` — many biological patterns are framed as target states toward which systems navigate, with a stronger hypothesis about pattern-space provenance.

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

All three address persistence of form, but they allocate explanatory work differently.

DSD shows that preserved outcomes can coexist with causal drift. Closure-of-constraints explains persistence through reciprocal maintenance of organization. Levin emphasizes goal states and adaptive navigation toward target morphology.

These accounts can sometimes coexist, but they are not interchangeable. A system that maintains itself through mutually dependent constraints is not thereby shown to represent or pursue a target state; a system that robustly reaches a target under perturbation may require more than self-maintenance.

## New distinction / contradiction

Keep separate:

1. **maintenance** — an organization keeps the conditions for its own continuation;
2. **restoration** — a system returns toward a state after perturbation;
3. **developmental convergence** — different starting states reach a similar phenotype;
4. **target representation** — some state functions as an internal or effective setpoint;
5. **pattern provenance** — where the specificity of that state ultimately comes from.

Evidence for one is not automatically evidence for the next.

## Pressure on the seed

Persistence may not require a single invariant stored or searched for by the system. It can sometimes be a property of reciprocal organization under turnover.

This weakens any version of the seed that treats stable form as evidence that a learner/system must first possess a target geometry.

## What remains unresolved

Which morphogenetic phenomena genuinely discriminate between maintenance, dynamical convergence, explicit/effective setpoints, and stronger pattern-space accounts.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Choose one perturbation-rich morphogenesis case. Before reading competing explanations, write predictions for:

- closure/self-maintenance;
- attractor/dynamical convergence;
- cybernetic target-state control;
- Levin's stronger pattern-space account.

Look specifically for interventions that preserve the constraint network while changing the putative target, or preserve the target while disrupting the constraint network. A useful probe must make at least two accounts expect different behavior.
