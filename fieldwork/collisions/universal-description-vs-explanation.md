# collision — universal description vs explanation

Status: **open**

## Independent inputs

- `hartl-et-al-2026-remapping-navigation` — proposes remapping/navigation plus error minimization as broad operational invariants of cognition across natural and artificial systems.
- `raja-et-al-2021-markov-blanket-trick` — argues that a formalism can acquire very broad scope through a representational trick without thereby explaining the processes it redescribes.
- `brooks-1991-intelligence-without-representation` and `chong-proctor-2020-affordances` — competent behavior can sometimes be explained through situated coupling rather than a central internal representational account.

## Why this is a collision rather than an agreement

The issue is not whether all of these systems can be *described* in a shared state/embedding/error language. The issue is what the shared description excludes and what mechanism or prediction it adds.

## New distinction / contradiction

Keep separate:

- **representability** — a phenomenon can be encoded in a framework;
- **compression/unification** — one vocabulary describes many cases;
- **mechanistic explanation** — the framework identifies what produces the phenomenon;
- **discrimination** — the framework rules out alternatives or predicts different outcomes;
- **intervention value** — the framework changes what experiment or action should be taken.

A framework can score high on the first two and low on the last three.

## Pressure on the seed

This is a hostile check against turning Geometry into a universal redescription. Any scale-free claim needs a failure class or discriminating consequence.

## What remains unresolved

Whether Hartl et al.'s framework can generate domain-specific predictions that weaker dynamical or representational vocabularies do not, and whether Geometry is intended as a mechanism at all rather than a private epistemic instrument.

## Candidate discriminating probe

Take one phenomenon that both a broad geometry/error-minimization account and a simpler domain-native mechanism can describe. Predeclare one intervention or prediction on which the two accounts differ. If none can be stated, classify the broad framework as organizational/redescriptive rather than explanatory for that case.
