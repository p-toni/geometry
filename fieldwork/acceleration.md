# accelerated research routing

Status: **operating model after round 001**

Round 001 showed that the expensive anti-collapse protocol is useful selectively, not by default. Research should now optimize for **wide throughput upstream and expensive proof obligations only near promotion**.

> Velocity happens before promotion, not instead of verification.

## Default routing

Every incoming question, source, claim, or anomaly starts in the cheapest lane that can answer it responsibly.

### Lane 1 — fast

Use ordinary research and synthesis.

Choose this lane when:

- the question is primarily explanatory or factual;
- one dominant mechanism is already well specified;
- the task does not materially depend on choosing among rival ontologies, system boundaries, objectives, or verification regimes;
- a strong answer can be checked directly against native sources or an executable/formal result.

Output:

```text
question → native research → answer / synthesis
```

Do not create scouts, branch taxonomies, collisions, or source-closed reconstructions merely because they are available.

Escalate only if the fast pass exposes a real conflation, unresolved rival, missing coordinate, or consequential claim that the current answer cannot safely carry.

### Lane 2 — escalation

Use a lightweight anti-collapse pass.

Choose this lane when the question plausibly bundles two or more of:

- distinct mechanisms;
- competing objectives or loss functions;
- explanatory levels;
- system boundaries;
- observation vs intervention vs control;
- representation vs constraint vs dynamics;
- incompatible verification regimes;
- live ontological alternatives.

Required moves:

1. state the native question;
2. name the bundled alternatives without translating them into Geometry vocabulary;
3. identify what decision or conclusion changes if they differ;
4. find the cheapest discriminator;
5. stop if the distinction is already sufficient.

Typical output is one short collision note or one probe contract, not a full research round.

### Lane 3 — deep

Open a full anti-collapse research cycle only when a strong escalation target exists.

A deep cycle requires at least one of:

- a substantive claim whose failure would change what we believe;
- a specific missing coordinate/observable that blocks formulation;
- a consequential decision with live rival models;
- a cross-domain generalization that is being considered for promotion.

Before opening the cycle, write:

```text
target:
strongest_rival:
discriminating_consequence:
verifier:
decision_changed_if_A:
decision_changed_if_B:
```

If those fields cannot be filled honestly, the deep lane is not earned.

## Promotion asymmetry

Exploration may be extremely wide. Human-facing and theory-facing promotion remains narrow.

```text
explore very wide
      ↓
triage very fast
      ↓
escalate selectively
      ↓
verify narrowly
      ↓
carry almost nothing
```

The cost profile should be asymmetric:

- asking another question is cheap;
- opening another source is cheap;
- recording a durable branch is somewhat expensive;
- promoting a collision is expensive;
- changing a working claim is very expensive;
- changing seed/canon is exceptional.

## Escalation triggers

Move upward one lane when a cheaper procedure cannot resolve a consequential uncertainty because:

- two explanations make materially different predictions;
- the result depends on an unstated objective;
- a system boundary changes the conclusion;
- a description can fit any outcome after the fact;
- an apparently universal claim is doing work;
- a coordinate/observable seems missing;
- the verifier and the generator share the same failure mode;
- the answer would be used to revise a durable Geometry commitment.

Do **not** escalate merely because a topic is intellectually deep.

## Stop rules

Acceleration depends as much on stopping as starting.

Stop the fast lane when the answer is adequate for the decision.

Stop escalation when one distinction or discriminator resolves the consequential ambiguity.

Stop a deep cycle when:

- the target fails;
- the target becomes non-discriminating;
- the missing coordinate is identified precisely enough to hand off;
- the decision no longer depends on the unresolved branch;
- source-closed reconstruction says no further test is earned.

Do not manufacture another target to preserve momentum.

## Relation to Geometry

Geometry is now the **research environment**, not the hypothesis every question must serve.

New questions may enter, resolve, and leave without changing Geometry. Cross-domain convergence is allowed to emerge only after independent native work and discriminating contact.

The post-round-001 toolkit remains a set of inquiry guards:

- use relative capacity rather than boundedness alone;
- specify preservation criteria before selecting the partition they justify;
- require mechanisms to rule out alternatives rather than redescribe outcomes;
- keep capability, explanatory level, system boundary, and verifier boundary explicit;
- preserve ontology choices until evidence discriminates them.

These guards route research. They are not a replacement theory.
