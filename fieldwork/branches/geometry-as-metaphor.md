# branch — when geometry is only metaphor

Role: **hostile**  
Status: open

## Native question

When do spatial or geometric descriptions of cognition, representation, agency, and learning provide explanatory or predictive value, and when do they merely redescribe relations in suggestive language?

## Search posture

Actively seek critiques of state-space metaphors, manifold language, representational geometry, cognitive maps, latent spaces, and universal dynamical descriptions.

Prefer work that names a discriminating failure: a case where geometric language hides causal mechanism, imports unjustified continuity, or makes an account too permissive to rule anything out.

## What would count against our seed

Evidence that the operations we call geometry can be replaced by a simpler non-geometric description with equal or greater predictive/interventional power, leaving 'geometry' as style rather than mechanism.
