# branch — institutional continuity

Role: **alien**  
Status: open

## Native question

How can an institution remain recognizably the same entity while its members, rules, precedents, goals, and environment change?

## Why this branch is here

This domain was chosen because persistence-under-change matters there, but its native literature does not need our vocabulary. It is a test of whether the research system can discover structure rather than search for echoes.

## Possible literatures

Legal identity, constitutional change, organizational routines, institutional memory, path dependence, common law, corporate succession, standards bodies.

## Anti-collapse rule

Do not begin by asking what the institution's 'geometry', 'interface', or 'invariants' are. Learn how the field itself explains continuity and rupture.

## What would make this branch matter

A native distinction about persistence, identity, or revision that survives translation and changes how we understand a bounded learner or system.
