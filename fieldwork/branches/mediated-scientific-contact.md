# branch — mediated scientific contact

Role: **hostile / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

How do instruments, inscriptions, routines, and argument transform local observations into claims that become stable enough to function as scientific facts?

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Laboratory studies, inscription devices, fact construction/stabilization, scientific practice, qualification, replication, records, instrumentation.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- social-construction accounts of fact stabilization;
- realist accounts where instruments mediate access to mind-independent structure;
- Bayesian/evidential accounts where increasing consistency of evidence explains the same stabilization without strong constructivism;
- institutional accounts emphasizing replication, criticism, and distributed error correction.

## What would make this branch matter

A reason to distinguish “the world answers” from the concrete channel through which an answer is produced, recorded, transported, checked, and made consequential.

## Stop condition

Pause if the branch starts collapsing into the claim that evidence is merely social. Preserve the difference between mediation of contact and denial of external constraint.
