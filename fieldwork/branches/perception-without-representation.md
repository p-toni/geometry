# branch — perception without internal representation

Role: **hostile**  
Status: open

## Native question

Can an organism perceive and act competently by directly exploiting structured information in its environment, without first constructing an internal representation of that environment?

## Why this branch exists

Parts of the current Geometry corpus treat understanding and action as if useful structure must become an internal navigable map. Ecological psychology and related non-representational approaches explicitly challenge that assumption.

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Direct perception, ecological information, affordances, organism–environment relation, sensorimotor coupling, enaction, representation, inference.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- Gibsonian ecological/direct perception;
- enactive approaches;
- inferential and representational perception;
- predictive-processing accounts;
- behavior-based robotics.

## What would make this branch matter to the seed

A case where competent prediction/action is better explained by environment-coupling than by an internal map, or a case where an internal representation is demonstrably necessary and the ecological account fails.

## Stop condition

If `representation` is weakened until every causal state counts as one, or `direct perception` is weakened until it is empirically indistinguishable from inferential accounts, the branch has stopped discriminating.
