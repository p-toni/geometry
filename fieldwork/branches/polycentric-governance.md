# branch — polycentric governance

Role: **alien / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

How can multiple partially autonomous decision centers coordinate around shared resources without collapsing into either one central controller or unconstrained local action?

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Polycentricity, common-pool resources, rules-in-use, institutional diversity, nested enterprises, monitoring, graduated sanctions, local fit, self-governance.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- centralized hierarchy;
- market coordination;
- polycentric governance;
- informal norms without durable institutional structure.

## What would make this branch matter

A native case where system-level persistence and correction depend on overlapping boundaries and differentiated authority rather than a single “learner” boundary.

## Stop condition

Pause when additional cases repeat the design-principle list without sharpening when polycentricity outperforms centralized or purely local alternatives.
