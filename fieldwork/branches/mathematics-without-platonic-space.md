# branch — mathematics without Platonic space

Role: **rival**  
Status: open

## Native question

What does it mean for mathematical structure to be objective or discoverable without positing a separately existing realm of mathematical objects or forms?

## Why this branch exists

Levin's argument uses mathematics as the entry point for a stronger ontology. Before treating mathematical discovery as evidence for that move, understand serious alternatives on their own terms.

## Families to keep separate

Structuralism, nominalism, formalism, fictionalism, indispensability arguments, modal accounts, and mathematical Platonism.

## Anti-collapse rule

Do not translate every position into 'different views of the same latent geometry'. Their disagreement about ontology and explanation is the point.

## What would make this branch matter to the seed

A clear distinction between structure being useful, structure being invariant across representations, structure being objectively constrained, and structure existing independently of physical or cognitive systems.
