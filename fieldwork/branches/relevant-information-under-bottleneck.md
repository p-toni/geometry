# branch — relevant information under a bottleneck

Role: **alien / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

How can a limited representation discard most of an input while preserving the information that is relevant to another variable or prediction task?

## Why this branch exists

Information theory gives a formal account of lossy representation under constraint without requiring cognitive, geometric, or metaphysical language.

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Rate-distortion theory, sufficient statistics, mutual information, information bottleneck, coding, distortion, relevance variables.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- task-specific lossy compression;
- generic source coding;
- predictive sufficiency;
- representation learned for downstream control rather than prediction.

## What would make this branch matter to the seed

A precise distinction between compression, relevance preservation, prediction, and understanding—especially if useful bounded representations do not need to preserve generic structure.

## Stop condition

Pause when additional papers only restate the same rate–relevance tradeoff without changing what counts as preserved information or how relevance is selected.
