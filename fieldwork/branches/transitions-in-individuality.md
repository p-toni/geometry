# branch — transitions in individuality

Role: **alien / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

How do entities that once replicated and competed independently become parts of a higher-level evolutionary individual?

## Why this branch exists

Major-transitions theory studies changes in individuality, cooperation, conflict mediation, division of labor, and information transmission without assuming that a larger agent or self was already present.

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Major evolutionary transitions, multilevel selection, cooperation, conflict, division of labor, mutual dependence, fitness reorganization, individuality.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- multilevel-selection accounts;
- cooperation / conflict-suppression accounts;
- ecological and life-history explanations;
- information-storage/transmission accounts;
- accounts that deny a single criterion of individuality.

## What would make this branch matter to the seed

A native account of when the relevant unit of persistence, adaptation, or responsibility changes scale—and whether that transition is gradual, operational, or observer-relative.

## Stop condition

Pause if 'individuality' becomes a generic synonym for boundary or coherence rather than the specific evolutionary problem of higher-level units becoming selectable and integrated.
