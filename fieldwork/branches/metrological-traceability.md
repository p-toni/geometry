# branch — metrological traceability

Role: **alien / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

What makes a measurement result comparable to a shared reference across instruments, laboratories, and time, and how is uncertainty propagated through that relation?

## Why this branch exists

Metrology treats measurement contact as a chain with explicit references, calibrations, uncertainty, and competence rather than as direct unmediated access to a quantity.

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Measurand, measurement result, calibration, metrological traceability, traceability chain, measurement standard, uncertainty budget, comparability, compatibility.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- direct instrument accuracy;
- calibration-chain / reference accounts;
- model-based measurement;
- conventional / operational accounts of quantities.

## What would make this branch matter

A precise reason that “the world answers” is insufficient unless the answer remains related to a reference through a documented channel whose transformations and uncertainties are known.

## Stop condition

Pause when additional material only restates traceability terminology without changing the relation among measurement, reference, uncertainty, and comparability.
