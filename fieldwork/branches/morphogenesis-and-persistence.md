# branch — morphogenesis and persistence

Role: **adjacent**  
Status: open

## Native question

How do developing and regenerating biological systems reach and maintain organism-level form despite perturbations, altered starting conditions, and unreliable parts?

## Why this branch exists

This question is independently important in developmental biology. It should be studied without assuming that anatomical targets are Platonic forms, internal representations, geometric objects, or metaphors.

## Native vocabulary to learn first

Morphogenesis, regeneration, developmental constraints, attractors, bioelectric signaling, pattern homeostasis, plasticity, robustness, target morphology, control theory as actually used by the literature.

## Competing families to preserve

- mechanistic / molecular accounts;
- dynamical-systems / attractor accounts;
- cybernetic / goal-state accounts;
- evolutionary-developmental accounts;
- Levin's stronger pattern-space account.

Do not merge them because they predict the same phenotype.

## What would make this branch matter to the seed

A result that distinguishes between locally encoded structure, dynamically reachable structure, and structure that requires a stronger account than either.
