# branch — dissipative order and boundary conditions

Role: **alien / cross-cutting**  
Status: open

## Native question

Under what conditions can stable or coherent macroscopic structures exist only because a system is maintained away from equilibrium through exchanges with its environment?

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Irreversible thermodynamics, dissipative structures, bifurcation, fluctuation, reaction–diffusion systems, boundary conditions, instability, far-from-equilibrium states.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- order as a dynamical consequence of driven nonlinear systems;
- order as a boundary-condition-sensitive bifurcation phenomenon;
- near-equilibrium thermodynamic accounts where such structures do not appear;
- later stochastic-thermodynamic accounts that do not require Prigogine's broader philosophical interpretation.

## What would make this branch matter

Evidence that the set of stable macroscopic forms is changed materially by throughput and boundary conditions, rather than being a fixed possibility space merely searched more or less efficiently.

## Stop condition

Pause when additional sources only restate self-organization language without sharpening the conditions, bifurcations, or environmental dependence that make specific structures possible.
