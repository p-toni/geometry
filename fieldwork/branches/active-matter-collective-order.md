# branch — active matter and collective order

Role: **alien**  
Status: open

## Native question

How do locally powered constituents generate collective order, flows, phase separation, defects, and instabilities without a central controller or equilibrium free-energy principle?

## Native vocabulary / literatures

Active matter, polar and nematic order, self-propelled particles, active hydrodynamics, dry/wet active systems, motility-induced phase separation, defects, nonequilibrium steady states.

## Competing explanations to preserve

- continuum hydrodynamic descriptions;
- semimicroscopic interaction models;
- equilibrium analogies that work only in limited regimes;
- system-specific biological mechanisms hidden by generic active-matter models.

## What would make this branch matter

A clean account of large-scale structure that emerges from local energy consumption and interactions without requiring representation, an explicit target, or a centralized comparison over possibilities.

## Stop condition

Pause if the branch only supplies another instance of generic self-organization without distinctions about energy injection, local interaction rules, or instability structure.
