# source — Cohn et al. 2007: reflections on clonal selection

Status: **stressed**  
Provenance: Melvin Cohn, N. Avrion Mitchison, William E. Paul, Arthur M. Silverstein, David W. Talmage & Martin Weigert, “Reflections on the clonal-selection theory,” *Nature Reviews Immunology* 7 (2007), 823–830. DOI: 10.1038/nri2177. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17893695/  
Branch: `generative-immune-repertoire`

## Native question

How can the immune system produce specific antibody responses to antigens that were not individually anticipated in advance?

## Claims in the source's own terms

The review revisits clonal-selection theory: lymphocyte clones carry receptors of differing specificities before exposure, antigen selectively activates matching clones, and the responding clones proliferate and differentiate. The explanatory move is selection from a diverse pre-existing repertoire rather than antigen instructing each antibody's detailed form.

Modern adaptive immunity adds generated receptor diversity, tolerance and later affinity-changing processes around this basic selection architecture.

## Evidence / argument actually offered

This is a historical and conceptual review written by major contributors to the field. It evaluates the development, successes and unresolved aspects of clonal-selection theory rather than reporting one experiment.

## Explicit limits / unknowns

- Clonal selection alone does not explain how receptor diversity is generated; V(D)J recombination and related mechanisms are separate machinery.
- Receptor cross-reactivity means “one clone, one antigen” is too simple.
- Recognition is not equivalent to a complete classification of antigen space.
- Self-tolerance and autoimmunity impose constraints on repertoire coverage.

## Assumptions under pressure

A bounded system need not explicitly encode every future challenge. It can maintain a diverse generative repertoire and amplify rare compatible responses after contact.

## Rival reading

The architecture is specific to adaptive immunity and should not be generalized into a universal exploration principle without demonstrating analogous generation, selection and amplification mechanisms elsewhere.

## Possible displacement of the seed

**Candidate displacement:** facing a huge possibility space may require neither storing all possibilities nor compressing them into one map. A third architecture is **generate broadly, test locally, amplify survivors**.
