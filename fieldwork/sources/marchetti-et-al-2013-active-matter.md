# source — Marchetti et al. 2013: hydrodynamics of soft active matter

Status: **stressed**  
Provenance: M. C. Marchetti et al., “Hydrodynamics of soft active matter,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* 85, 1143–1189 (2013). DOI: 10.1103/RevModPhys.85.1143. https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.85.1143  
Branch: `active-matter-collective-order`

## Native question

How should the collective behavior of many locally driven constituents be described when the system is intrinsically out of equilibrium?

## Claims in the source's own terms

Active matter consists of components that consume energy locally and convert it into motion or mechanical stress. Large ensembles can display polar or nematic order, moving bands, phase separation, defects, and large-scale instabilities.

The review develops continuum and semimicroscopic descriptions and distinguishes “dry” systems, where substrate friction removes momentum conservation, from “wet” suspensions coupled to fluid flow.

Collective structure can therefore arise without centralized control and without an equilibrium free-energy principle specifying the final state.

## Evidence / argument actually offered

The review integrates theoretical derivations with experimental systems including bacterial suspensions, cytoskeletal systems, motile organisms, and vibrated granular matter.

## Explicit limits / unknowns

- Generic continuum models deliberately suppress many biological details.
- Similar collective patterns across systems do not imply identical microscopic mechanisms.
- “Active matter” is a physical category, not evidence that cognition or agency reduces to collective dynamics.

## Rival reading

The branch may be useful precisely because it explains apparent organization without representational or teleological concepts. Importing cognitive language into these systems would add claims not required by the physics.

## Possible displacement

**Candidate displacement:** coherent large-scale organization does not by itself license an internal representation, target, learner, or navigator. Some forms of order are consequences of driven local dynamics and conservation/symmetry structure alone.
