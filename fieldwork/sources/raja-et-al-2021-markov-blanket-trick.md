# source — Raja et al. 2021: the Markov blanket trick

Status: **stressed**  
Provenance: Vicente Raja, Dinesh Valluri, Edward Baggs, Anthony Chemero & Michael L. Anderson, “The Markov blanket trick: On the scope of the free energy principle and active inference,” *Physics of Life Reviews* 39, 2021, 49–72. DOI: 10.1016/j.plrev.2021.09.001. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34563472/  
Branch: `universal-framework-critique`

## Native question

Do the free-energy principle and active inference genuinely explain biological/cognitive organization at universal scope, or does the Markov-blanket formalism mainly provide a very general redescription?

## Claims in the source's own terms

The authors challenge the claim that the free-energy principle is a universal biological/cognitive principle and that active inference is an all-encompassing process theory. They argue that Markov blankets enable approximate Bayesian inference to be generalized very broadly—the “Markov blanket trick”—and that active inference can presuppose successful perception and action rather than explain how those capacities arise.

They propose treating the free-energy framework more modestly as one modeling framework among alternatives unless additional explanatory work is earned.

## Evidence / argument actually offered

The paper provides a conceptual and formal analysis of the free-energy/Markov-blanket derivation and compares the resulting scope claims with what is required of a process explanation.

## Explicit limits / unknowns

- This is a critical philosophical/formal analysis, not an empirical refutation of every active-inference model.
- Particular active-inference models can still be empirically productive.
- Defenders dispute the critique and can add process-level commitments not captured by the most abstract formalism.

## Assumptions under pressure

Universal redescribability is not by itself explanatory discrimination. A framework that accommodates nearly everything needs additional constraints to tell us why this system does this rather than something else.

## Rival reading

The breadth of FEP may be a feature: a general variational principle can organize diverse process models without itself being the whole mechanism. Critiquing the shell does not invalidate specific models built inside it.

## Possible displacement of the seed

**Candidate displacement:** any future claim that remapping/navigation/error minimization or “geometry” is a universal principle needs an explicit answer to: what systems or outcomes does the principle rule out, and what prediction/intervention follows that a weaker redescription would not provide?
