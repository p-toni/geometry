Macroscopic is the largest version of the pattern: cognition as a living system, not a chat surface.

Its center is a cognitive kernel that keeps events, context, durable memory, missions, artifacts, and approvals separate enough to be trusted.

> [thesis|product bet]
> The useful assistant is not louder. It is better at knowing what should surface, what should stay latent, and what needs permission before it acts.
